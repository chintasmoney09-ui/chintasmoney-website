Why your net profit is smaller than you think

Every trade has a hidden cost stack: brokerage (0.03% or ₹20/order for intraday at most discount brokers, often free for delivery), STT, exchange transaction charges, 18% GST on brokerage + exchange charges, SEBI charges, and stamp duty on the buy side. On small moves these can turn a "winning" trade into a loss. Knowing your breakeven before you enter is pure discipline — and discipline is what ChintasMoney measures.
