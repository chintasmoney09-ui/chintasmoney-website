Nifty vs Sensex: what's the difference?

Reading time: ~4 min · Educational — not investment advice

Two numbers every Indian trader hears daily. They tell a similar story in slightly different ways.

Nifty 50

The flagship index of the NSE (National Stock Exchange). It tracks 50 of India's largest, most-traded companies across sectors — a broad snapshot of the market.

Sensex

The flagship index of the BSE (Bombay Stock Exchange). It tracks 30 large, established companies. "Sensex" = Sensitive Index, and it's India's oldest market index.

So what's the real difference?

  • Exchange: Nifty = NSE; Sensex = BSE.
  • Number of stocks: Nifty = 50; Sensex = 30.
  • Breadth: Nifty is a touch broader; both are large-cap barometers.

Because they hold many of the same giant companies, they usually move together. Derivatives traders watch index lot sizes for both (and Bank Nifty).
