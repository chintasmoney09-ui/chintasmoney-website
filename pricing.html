FAQ

Straight answers

What exactly does ChintasMoney do?

You log your trades. It calculates a Discipline Score, names your trader personality, ranks your repeating mistakes, tracks streaks & badges, and gives you free risk tools — so you improve your behaviour, which is what actually decides your P&L.

Do you give buy/sell tips?

No. Never. Zero trading calls, tips, or investment advice. It's a self-awareness & journaling tool that mirrors your behaviour — nothing more.

Do I need to connect my broker?

No. You log trades yourself or import a CSV. Your data, your mirror. No broker login, no market-data fees.

How is the Discipline Score calculated?

Purely from your logged trades — did you set a stop-loss, exit on plan, size risk sensibly, avoid overtrading and revenge/FOMO exits? Each trade earns or loses points; the score (0–100) is the honest average your P&L can't fake. The more you log, the sharper it gets.

What markets does it work for?

Built for Indian F&O, intraday & swing traders — index & stock options, futures and equities. The free live charts also cover gold, crude, crypto, global indices and forex. You can log any instrument; behaviour is behaviour.

How does the business make money?

Simple subscriptions — Free forever, Go Plus ₹199/mo, Platinum ₹499/mo and Diamond ₹999/mo, plus optional token top-ups, with a 3-day trial. We sell intelligence and clarity, never your data, and never trading products.

How do tokens work?

Premium actions run on tokens: logging a trade costs 30, the AI coach 30, a Trade Replay 30, a deep-dive 50. Calculators are always free. Every account gets a free 30-token daily bonus. Plans include a monthly token grant — Free 150, Go Plus 600, Platinum 1200, Diamond 3000 — and you can top up anytime (400/₹199, 700/₹499, 1600/₹999, 3000/₹2000). Plan tokens reset monthly; top-up tokens are valid 2 months. The AI Discipline Coach is available on Diamond.

Is my data safe?

In this version your journal stays on your own device — nothing is uploaded unless you opt into cloud sync later. We never sell your data and there are no ads. See our Privacy Policy.

Is this SEBI-registered advice?

No. ChintasMoney is an educational journaling tool — it gives no tips, calls or investment advice and is not a SEBI-registered adviser. F&O trading is risky and most traders lose money. See our Risk Disclaimer.
