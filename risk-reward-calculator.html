What is a good risk-reward ratio?

A risk-reward ratio compares how much you stand to lose against how much you stand to gain. At 1:2, you risk ₹1 to make ₹2 — so you can be wrong more than half the time and still come out ahead. Anything below 1:1 means your winners have to land far more often than your losers just to break even, which is a hard way to trade.

Why it matters more than being "right"

Most traders obsess over win rate. But a trader who wins 40% of the time at 1:3 crushes a trader who wins 60% at 1:1. Risk-reward — not prediction — is what makes a strategy survive.
