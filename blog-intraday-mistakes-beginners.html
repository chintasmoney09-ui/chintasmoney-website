10 intraday trading mistakes beginners make

Reading time: ~5 min · Educational — not investment advice

Intraday looks simple and punishes fast. Most beginners don't lose because they picked the "wrong stock" — they lose to the same handful of habits, over and over. Here are the 10 most common intraday trading mistakes, and the one-line fix for each.

1. Trading without a stop-loss

The #1 account-killer. Fix: decide your exit before you enter — every single trade.

2. Oversized positions

Risking too much on one trade. Fix: the 1% risk rule — never risk more than 1–2% of capital per trade.

3. Revenge trading after a loss

Trying to "win it back" instantly. Fix: after a red trade, step away 10 minutes. More here.

4. Overtrading out of boredom

Taking trades just to be "in the market." Fix: a daily cap on number of trades; boredom is not a setup.

5. Chasing (FOMO)

Jumping into a move that's already run. Fix: if it's not your setup, skip it.

6. Cutting winners early, holding losers

Fear books gains fast; hope lets losses run. Fix: pre-set target and stop, honour both.

7. No trading plan

Entering on gut feel. Fix: write your setup, entry, stop and target before the session.

8. Ignoring costs

Brokerage, STT and slippage eat overtraders alive. Fix: fewer, higher-quality trades.

9. Trading on tips & Telegram calls

Outsourcing your decisions. Fix: follow your own tested rules; nobody cares about your capital like you do.

10. Not reviewing trades

Repeating mistakes because they're invisible. Fix: keep a trading journal and review it weekly.
