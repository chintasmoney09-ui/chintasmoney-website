Brokerage & trading charges in India, explained

Reading time: ~5 min · Educational — not investment advice

The "₹20 per order" you see is only part of the story. A pile of small charges rides on every trade, and for active traders they add up to real money. Here's what you actually pay.

The charges on a typical trade

  • Brokerage: the broker's fee (flat per order, or a %).
  • STT (Securities Transaction Tax): a government tax on transactions — notable on options and intraday.
  • Exchange transaction charges: NSE/BSE fees.
  • GST: 18% on (brokerage + exchange charges).
  • SEBI charges + stamp duty: small, but present.
  • DP charges: on delivery sells, from the depository.

Why it quietly bleeds you

Each cost is tiny, but they hit every single trade. Trade 20 times a day and the house takes a cut 20 times — before you've made a rupee. This is a core reason overtrading is so deadly: you lose to costs even on "flat" days.
