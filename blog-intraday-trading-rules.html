10 intraday trading rules for beginners

Reading time: ~5 min · Educational — not investment advice

Intraday trading rewards discipline and punishes impulse — fast. These aren't tips or calls; they're the behavioural rules that keep beginners in the game long enough to actually learn. Screenshot them.

The 10 rules

  1. Never trade without a stop-loss. Decide your exit before you enter. "I'll watch it" is how small losses become account-ending ones.
  2. Risk ≤1% of capital per trade. Size the position to the stop, not the stop to the position. See the 1% rule.
  3. No revenge trading. After a loss, step away — don't fire back to "win it back." Here's how to break the loop.
  4. Set a daily loss limit. Hit it and you're done for the day. Protecting the account beats chasing a comeback.
  5. Don't overtrade. Boredom is not a setup. More trades = more fees + more mistakes.
  6. Trade your plan, not the noise. No plan = no trade. Tips from Telegram are not a plan.
  7. Book losers fast, let winners run. Most beginners do the opposite — cut winners early, hope on losers.
  8. Avoid the first few minutes. The open is violent; many pros wait for it to settle. See the best time to trade intraday.
  9. One position at a time (at first). You can't manage five trades and your emotions together.
  10. Log every trade. Entry, stop, exit reason, and the emotion behind it — that record is where improvement comes from.

Why rules beat "strategies" for beginners

Everyone hunts for the perfect strategy; almost nobody loses because their strategy was bad. They lose because they broke their own rules — moved a stop, doubled down, overtraded after a red candle. Rules are the strategy that actually matters first.
