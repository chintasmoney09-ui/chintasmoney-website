About

Why ChintasMoney exists

The honest mirror your broker never shows you.

Most Indian F&O traders lose — and almost none of them lose to the market. They lose to themselves: the revenge trade after a red day, the missing stop-loss, the exit taken on emotion instead of plan. Your broker shows you the damage in your P&L. It never shows you the reason.

ChintasMoney is a Trader Report Card. You log your own past trades and get a brutally honest Discipline Score — your trading personality, your best setups, and the exact habits quietly draining your account. It's a mirror for your behaviour, built so you can trade calmer and keep more of what you make.

What we are — and what we're not

  • ✅ An educational behaviour-analysis tool for your own past trades.
  • ✅ Free tools (risk calculator, live charts) with no sign-up.
  • 🚫 Not investment advice. We give no tips, calls or buy/sell signals.
  • 🚫 Not a predictor of future trades, and not SEBI-registered advice.

Our only business is helping you build discipline — never selling you trades. See our risk disclaimer.
