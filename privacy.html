Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ChintasMoney ("we", "us") handles information when you use our website and app (the "Service"). We are built privacy-first: your trading data is yours.

1. Information we handle

  • Your trade logs & journal. In the current version, the trades, notes and settings you enter are stored locally in your browser/device (local storage). They are not uploaded to us unless and until you explicitly create an account and enable cloud sync (a future, opt-in feature).
  • Account details (if you sign up in future): name, email and a public handle you choose.
  • Usage & device data. Basic, aggregated analytics (pages visited, rough location by IP, device type) to improve the Service.
  • Support messages. If you email us, we keep that correspondence to help you.

2. What we do NOT do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not access your broker account or place trades.
  • We do not need your PAN, bank or demat details to use the free journaling features.

3. How we use information

To provide and improve the Service, calculate your Discipline Score and analytics from data you enter, respond to support, process subscriptions (via our payment partner), and keep the Service secure.

4. Payments

Paid subscriptions are processed by a third-party payment gateway. We do not store your full card details on our servers; the gateway handles them under its own security standards.

5. Cookies & analytics

We use minimal cookies/local storage for functionality and privacy-friendly analytics. You can clear these anytime from your browser settings.

6. Your choices & rights

  • Access & delete: because your journal lives on your device, you can delete it anytime by clearing app data. If you create a cloud account, you can request export or deletion by emailing us.
  • You can opt out of non-essential emails at any time.

7. Children

The Service is intended for users aged 18 and above. It is not directed at children.

8. Changes

We may update this policy; material changes will be posted here with a new date.

9. Contact

Questions? Email support@chintasmoney.com.
