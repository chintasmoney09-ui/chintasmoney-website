Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: 14 September 2026

We want you to try ChintasMoney risk-free and stay only if it helps.

1. Free plan & trial

The Free plan is free forever for the features listed on our pricing page. Paid plans may include a free trial — you won't be charged during a stated trial period, and you can cancel before it ends at no cost.

2. Cancellations

You can cancel a paid subscription at any time from your account or by emailing support@chintasmoney.com. After cancellation, you keep access until the end of the current billing period; it will not renew after that.

3. Refunds

  • 7-day money-back: if you're not satisfied with a paid plan, email us within 7 days of your first payment for that plan and we'll refund it in full.
  • After 7 days, payments for the current period are generally non-refundable, but we review genuine issues (e.g. accidental duplicate charges) case by case.
  • One-time token top-ups are non-refundable once the tokens have been used.

4. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via our payment gateway, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

5. Contact

Email support@chintasmoney.com with your registered email and payment reference and we'll help quickly.
