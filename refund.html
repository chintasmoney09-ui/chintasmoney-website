Refund & Cancellation

Last updated: 30 September 2026

ChintasMoney is a token-based educational tool. Please read this policy carefully before paying — by making a payment you agree to it.

1. Free plan, daily bonus & trial

The Free plan is free forever for the features on our pricing page, and every account gets a free 30-token daily bonus (enough to log one trade a day). Paid plans may include a short trial (currently 3 days) — cancel before it ends at no cost.

2. How tokens work

Premium actions (logging a trade, the AI coach, Trade Replay, deep-dives) run on tokens. Each plan includes a monthly token grant; you can also buy top-up token packs. Plan tokens reset monthly (use-it-or-lose-it). Top-up tokens are valid for 2 months from purchase, after which any unused balance expires. Tokens have no cash value and are never exchangeable for money.

3. Cancellations

You can cancel a paid plan at any time from your account or by emailing support@chintasmoney.com. After cancellation you keep access for the period already paid; it will not renew.

4. Refunds — 3-day window

  • 3-day refund window: if a paid plan isn't for you, email us within 3 days (72 hours) of your first payment for that plan and we'll refund it in full. This is the only refund window.
  • After 3 days, all payments are non-refundable — plans and token top-ups alike. Because we deliver software, tools and tokens immediately and they cost real money to build and run, we do not provide refunds after this window.
  • Tokens are non-refundable once granted, and a payment is non-refundable once its tokens have been used or forwarded, even within the 3-day window.
  • We still review genuine billing errors (e.g. an accidental duplicate charge) case by case.

5. How refunds are processed

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method via our payment gateway, typically within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

6. Contact

Email support@chintasmoney.com with your registered email and payment reference and we'll help quickly.
