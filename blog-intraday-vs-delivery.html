Intraday vs delivery trading: the difference

Reading time: ~4 min · Educational — not investment advice

Same stock, two completely different games. The difference comes down to one thing: do you close the trade the same day, or hold it?

Intraday (MIS)

  • Holding: opened and closed the same day — nothing carries overnight.
  • Margin: higher leverage, so small moves swing your account hard.
  • Risk: fast, high-pressure; auto square-off near close.
  • Tax: intraday equity is speculative business income.

Delivery (CNC)

  • Holding: you take delivery and hold for days, months or years.
  • Margin: usually pay full price; no overnight leverage risk from the broker.
  • Risk: slower pace, but you carry overnight/gap risk.
  • Tax: treated as capital gains (short or long term).

Which should you do?

Intraday needs full-time focus and strong nerves; delivery suits patience and a longer view. Neither is "better" — pick the one you can do with discipline. Related: swing vs intraday.

The style doesn't matter if the habits are broken

Intraday or delivery, the same leaks sink traders: no stop, oversizing, revenge. ChintasMoney scores your discipline either way.
