FAQ

Straight answers

What exactly does ChintasMoney do?

You log your trades. It calculates a Discipline Score, names your trader personality, ranks your repeating mistakes, tracks streaks & badges, and gives you free risk tools — so you improve your behaviour, which is what actually decides your P&L.

Do you give buy/sell tips?

No. Never. Zero trading calls, tips, or investment advice. It's a self-awareness & journaling tool that mirrors your behaviour — nothing more.

Do I need to connect my broker?

No. You log trades yourself or import a CSV. Your data, your mirror. No broker login, no market-data fees.

How does the business make money?

Simple subscriptions — Free forever, Plus ₹199/mo, Pro ₹499/mo, with a 7-day Pro trial. We sell intelligence and clarity, never your data, and never trading products.

Is my data safe?

In this version your data stays on your own device. Nothing is sold and there are no ads.

Is this SEBI-registered advice?

No. It is not investment advice and makes no return claims. F&O trading is risky and most traders lose money.
