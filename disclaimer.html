1. No advice, no tips

ChintasMoney does not provide buy/sell recommendations, tips, calls, or portfolio management. Everything in the Service is for education and self-reflection based on data you enter yourself.

2. Trading is risky

Trading and investing — especially in Futures & Options (F&O) — involve a high risk of loss and are not suitable for everyone. As highlighted by SEBI studies, the large majority of individual F&O traders lose money. You can lose more than you expect. Never trade with money you cannot afford to lose.

3. Tools are educational

The Discipline Score, risk/position-size calculator, projections in the Dream Planner, and any figures shown are illustrative, based on assumptions and the data you provide. They are not guarantees of any outcome and past behaviour does not predict future results.

4. Market data

Charts and quotes are provided by third parties (e.g. TradingView) and may be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate. Do not rely on them for real-time execution decisions.

5. Your responsibility

All trading decisions are yours alone. Please consult a SEBI-registered investment adviser and do your own research before making any financial decision.

6. Nature of our business

ChintasMoney is an educational software product (SaaS) — a trading journal and behaviour-analytics tool that analyses a user's own past, self-entered trades. We are not a trading-signals or tips service, not an investment/financial advisory, not a broker or portfolio manager, and we do not deal in forex, binary options, securities or any financial instruments. We sell software subscriptions only. Our motto is simple: reduce your losses by understanding your behaviour.
