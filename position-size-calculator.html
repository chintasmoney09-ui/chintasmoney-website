How to calculate position size

Position sizing has three steps: (1) decide your risk amount = capital × risk %. (2) Find your per-share risk = entry price − stop-loss. (3) Quantity = risk amount ÷ per-share risk. Example: ₹1,00,000 capital, 1% risk = ₹1,000 to lose. Entry ₹500, stop ₹490 = ₹10 risk per share. Quantity = 1,000 ÷ 10 = 100 shares.

How much should you risk per trade?

Risk 1% and you can be wrong 20 times in a row and survive. Risk 10% and one bad day can end you. Position sizing — not prediction — is what keeps traders alive.
