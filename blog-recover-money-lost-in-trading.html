Recovery

Lost money in trading? Reduce your losses by understanding your behaviour

Reading time: ~4 min · Educational — not investment advice

First, the honest part: no website, tip, or "recovery expert" can magically get your lost money back — and anyone promising that is a scam. What you can do is stop losing the same way again. Our whole motto is simple: reduce your losses by understanding your behaviour. Fix the habits that caused the damage, and the bleeding stops. Here's the real path.

Step 1 — Stop the bleed

The biggest damage after a loss comes from revenge trading — bigger size, no plan, trying to recover fast. It almost always deepens the hole. Before anything else: reduce your size dramatically or step away for a few days. You can't rebuild while still bleeding. More on stopping revenge trading.

Step 2 — Find out exactly why you lost

"The market went against me" is not a reason. Go back through your recent trades and tag each one: did you have a stop? Did you exit on plan or on emotion? Was it FOMO? Almost every blown account traces to the same five mistakes — and you probably repeat just two of them. Name them and you're halfway back.

Step 3 — Rebuild with tiny risk

Recovery is a marathon, not a revenge sprint. Use the 1% risk rule so no single trade can hurt you again, and rebuild confidence with small, disciplined trades. Ten clean trades at small size are worth more than one lucky big one.

Step 4 — Measure discipline, not P&L

While recovering, judge yourself on whether you followed your rules, not on the day's profit. A red day where you respected every stop is a good day. A green day from a reckless trade is a warning. This mindset shift is what separates traders who come back from those who blow up twice.
